# ShieldWave Claims Handling Runbook (Sample)

## 1. Standard Claims Intake Process

1. Policyholder reports loss via phone, app, or web portal.
2. Claim is assigned a claim number and routed to an adjuster within 1
   business day.
3. Adjuster contacts policyholder within 2 business days to schedule
   inspection or request documentation.
4. Documentation required: photos of damage, proof of ownership for
   damaged items over $500, repair estimates from a licensed contractor.

## 2. Auto Claims — At-Fault Determination

For auto claims involving another party, ShieldWave uses a comparative
negligence standard consistent with state law. The adjuster's fault
determination is documented in the claim file and can be appealed by either
party within 30 days.

## 3. Escalation to Senior Adjuster

A claim is escalated to a senior adjuster when:
- The claimed loss exceeds $25,000, or
- The claim involves a coverage dispute (e.g., disputed exclusion), or
- The claim is tied to a state-declared disaster (these often involve
  non-standard filing deadlines and documentation requirements set by the
  state insurance department, not by ShieldWave's internal policy).

## 4. What This Runbook Does NOT Cover

This runbook does not track state-specific regulatory deadlines, disaster
declarations, or emergency filing extensions — those change over time and
must be checked against current state insurance department sources at the
time a claim is handled.
