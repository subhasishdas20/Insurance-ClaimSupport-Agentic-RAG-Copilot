# ShieldWave Standard Homeowner Policy — Coverage Wording (Sample)

## Section 3: Water Damage

### 3.1 Sudden and Accidental Discharge
Damage caused by the sudden and accidental discharge of water from a
plumbing, heating, or air conditioning system — including a burst pipe — is
**covered** under Section 3.1, subject to the standard $1,000 deductible.
Coverage applies to the resulting damage to the dwelling and personal
property, not to the cost of repairing or replacing the pipe itself (see
Exclusion 3.4).

### 3.2 Flood Exclusion
Damage caused by flood, surface water, or water that backs up from a sewer
or drain **is excluded** from this policy. Flood coverage requires a
separate National Flood Insurance Program (NFIP) policy or private flood
endorsement.

### 3.3 Gradual Leakage Exclusion
Damage resulting from continuous or repeated seepage of water occurring
over a period of 14 days or more is excluded, regardless of cause.

### 3.4 Exclusion — Cost of the Failed Component
The cost to repair or replace the pipe, fixture, or appliance that caused
the discharge is not covered. Only the resulting property damage is
covered.

## Section 5: Deductibles

| Peril | Standard Deductible |
|---|---|
| Water damage (sudden/accidental) | $1,000 |
| Wind/hail | $2,500 or 2% of dwelling coverage, whichever is greater |
| Theft | $500 |
| Fire | $1,000 |

## Section 8: Claims Filing Timeline (Internal Standard)

Policyholders must report a claim within 60 days of the date of loss under
standard policy terms. Note: this internal standard may be superseded by
state-mandated extensions following a declared disaster — those extensions
are not reflected in this document and must be verified against current
state insurance department guidance at the time of filing.
